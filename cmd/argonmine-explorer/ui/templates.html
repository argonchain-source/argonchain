How it works

1
Choose Pool on the Mine page of the app. Your miner gets the same blocks as a solo miner, except that the reward goes to the pool address below.
2
Your miner reports a share about every {{.Data.Stats.ShareInterval}} seconds: proof of the hashes it did, checked one by one here. The pool keeps the most recent shares worth two blocks of work, the way most pools do (pay per last N shares).
3
When a pool block has {{num .Data.Maturity}} confirmations, its reward less the {{.Data.Stats.FeePercent}}% fee is split over the shares in that window, by weight. Balances of {{coin .Data.Stats.MinPayout}} {{.Coin}} or more are paid out automatically within minutes.

Finding a block is random: the pool's expected wait is a block's work divided by the pool's hashrate, and a round can run well past it. Blocks that end up off the main chain pay nothing; their shares stay in the window for the next one. The pool holds only what is on its way to miners. The pool address is {{.Data.Stats.Address}}, and the complete ledger of shares, balances and payouts is at /api/pool/ledger.
