      How it works

      Start in three steps

      1. 01

        Create a wallet

        Save your 24 words and set a password.

      2. 02

        Start mining

        Blocks you find pay their reward to you, less 1% for the treasury from block 300.

      3. 03

        Send and swap

        Pay any {{.Brand}} address or swap coins.

      Design

      Why {{.Brand}}

      Ordinary hardware

      Memory-hard hashing narrows the lead of specialised machines.

      Fair launch

      No premine or sale. From block 300, 1% of each block reward goes to the treasury.

      Self-custody

      Your keys stay on your device.

      Bitcoin's supply schedule

      50 ARG per block, halving every 210,000 blocks.

      No scripting

      Payments and assets only.

      Published specification

      The consensus rules and security review are public.

      Coins

      ARG, wARG and uARG

      ARGThe mined coin

      Created only by mining. Used for network fees.

      Supply21,000,000
      wARGWrapped ARG

      Wrapped one for one by the swap service, which holds the ARG.

      Value1 wARG = 1 ARG
      uARGDollar token

      Can be withdrawn one for one as USDC or USDT through the bridge.

      Value1 uARG ≈ $1
